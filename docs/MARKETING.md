# Field Card Delivery and Measurement marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Independent publishers and privacy-minded developers. **Offer.** Let a publisher distinguish a delivered file from a button click.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Walk through one synthetic delivery trace and the aggregate it changes. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Reproduce the consent and delivery tests. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 3 independent test runs. |

### Sales test

Use a reproducible synthetic report as the sales demo for a publisher setup workshop. Target one written scope with a confirmed budget. Qualify one publisher interested in paid implementation support. Analytics volume itself is not revenue.

### Measurement and decision

Use `field-measurement-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** No visitor rows, private reports or production authentication bindings are included. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
